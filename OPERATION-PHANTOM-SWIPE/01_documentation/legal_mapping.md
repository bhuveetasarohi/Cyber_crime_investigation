**Current-law context:** The IPC was replaced by the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS), effective 1 July 2024. Include IPC provisions only as historical/reference mapping where the assignment requires them, and independently verify corresponding BNS provisions and commencement/transition issues against official sources. Do not assume a one-to-one section equivalence.

**Electronic evidence:** Discuss the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, especially Sections 61–63, concerning electronic/digital records and admissibility. A hash alone does not establish admissibility; lawful collection, provenance, integrity, documentation and applicable statutory requirements matter.

**Budapest Convention comparative framework:** Articles 2 (illegal access), 3 (illegal interception), 4 (data interference), 7 (computer-related forgery), 8 (computer-related fraud), 14 (procedural powers), 16 (expedited preservation), 18 (production orders) and 23 (international cooperation) may be discussed comparatively. India is not a party; do not describe these provisions as directly binding treaty obligations on India.
